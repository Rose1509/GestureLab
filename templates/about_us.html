About Gesture Lab

Gesture Lab is a platform dedicated to making sign language accessible and fun for everyone. We use cutting-edge AI to give you real-time feedback and personalized practice, so you can build skills quickly and confidently—whether you’re learning for yourself, your job, or to connect with the deaf and hard-of-hearing community.

Our lessons, practice tools, and progress tracking are designed to fit your pace. Learn the basics, master finger-spelling, and move on to everyday signs and conversations—all in one place.

Our Mission

We’re here to bridge communication gaps by empowering people to learn sign language. We believe in an inclusive, supportive, and effective learning experience that connects learners and communities—so everyone can communicate with confidence.
